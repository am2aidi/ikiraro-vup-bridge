# Isoko Bridge demo video plan

The NISR webinar asks for a screen recording that shows the app and its main features. Keep the finished video under 3 minutes. Record the app itself on a computer; do not film the screen with a phone. This guide is a plan, not a recorded video.

## Before recording

- Use a clean browser profile or reset the demo data so personal browsing details do not appear.
- Use only the sample accounts below. Do not show real household records, passwords, phone numbers, or payment details.
- Make sure the public demo link loads and the text is readable at the recording size.
- Have the NISR analysis page ready. If showing an order or export, use demo data and state that no real money moves.
- Record with desktop screen-capture software and export WebM or GIF. Keep the final file at or below 3 minutes.

## Suggested 2 minute 40 second walkthrough

| Time | Show | Suggested narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Open the public app and show its name and role menu. | “Isoko Bridge is a student prototype exploring how local food access could help while a VUP payment is delayed. All payments and balances here are simulated.” |
| 0:15–0:40 | Sign in as a VUP member. Browse the food market, add one item to the cart, and show the cart count. | “A member can browse food from demo local sellers, review the cart, and choose how to pay.” |
| 0:40–1:05 | Open checkout and show cash, MoMo, Airtel Money, bank/SACCO, and the member’s wallet or available food credit. Do not enter real payment details. | “Digital methods are demos and do not contact a provider. Food credit uses the demo VUP amount owed minus open food credit. No planner approval is required.” |
| 1:05–1:45 | Open NISR Analysis. Show the national summary, programme or province comparison, a district table, and the map. | “The analysis uses prepared 2023–24 EICV7 summary values. The current figures are provisional. They show group patterns, not the reason for an individual delay or a family’s current payment status.” |
| 1:45–2:10 | Change the prototype activity period, then point out the label separating demo orders from the fixed NISR baseline. | “This date filter changes only simulated prototype activity. It does not change the NISR survey period.” |
| 2:10–2:30 | Show the analysis CSV or print/save report control, then show the admin work-record table without changing values. | “Reports can be downloaded for review. Admins manage demo work records and money actions; planners can help with cases.” |
| 2:30–2:40 | End on the limitations or source note. | “This is not a live government, bank, SACCO, or mobile-money service. The prepared summaries still need final weighting and validation.” |

## Before adding the video to GitHub

- Watch the full recording and confirm the audio, labels, totals, and role permissions are clear.
- Confirm it is no longer than 3 minutes and does not expose personal information.
- Add the real recording under `docs/` (for example, `docs/isoko-bridge-demo.webm`) and link it from the README.
- Do not add this script as if it were the required video; the actual screen recording is still needed.
