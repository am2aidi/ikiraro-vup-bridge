## 1. Declaration of Originality & Sources
We hereby declare that this submission is our own original work produced for the NISR 2026 Big Data Hackathon. It has not been previously submitted, in whole or in part, to any other competition, conference, or academic qualification.

All secondary data sources, methodologies, software libraries, and third-party assets utilized in this project are explicitly acknowledged and referenced below:
- **Primary Data:** National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) Vision 2020 Umurenge Programme (VUP) Household Survey Microdata, accessed via the [NISR Microdata Portal](https://microdata.statistics.gov.rw/index.php/catalog).

- **National Policy Alignment:** Second National Strategy for Transformation (NST2) and Rwanda Vision 2050.
- **Open-Source Software Libraries:** Documented in `requirements.txt` and `package.json`.
