# Demo Video Guide & Presentation Script

This directory is designated for the official 3-minute hackathon demonstration video (`demo.webm` or `demo.gif`) as required by the NISR 2026 Big Data Hackathon guidelines.

## Recording Guidelines
- **Duration:** Strictly 3 minutes or less.
- **Recording Mode:** Browser screen recording on a laptop/desktop (e.g. Screencastify, OBS Studio, or Windows Game Bar / Snipping Tool).
- **Format:** `.webm` or `.gif` (or `.mp4`).

---

## 3-Minute Presentation Script & Flow

### 0:00 – 0:20 | Introduction & The Problem
- **Narrative:** "Welcome to Isoko Bridge. In Rwanda, over 3,000 surveyed households receive Vision 2020 Umurenge Programme support. However, NISR microdata reveals that disbursements arrive an average of 9.2 days late, with over 52% of families waiting more than a week. During this gap, families struggle to buy food, while local farmers struggle for buyers."
- **Visual:** Landing page showing the problem statement and NISR headline statistics.

### 0:20 – 1:00 | Planner Intelligence & Evidence Dashboards
- **Narrative:** "Logging in as a Government Planner, we view NISR data in action. We see delay distributions across Direct Support, Public Works, and NSDS. In our interactive 30-district choropleth map, we spot high-friction hotspots like Gisagara and Kirehe, where delays exceed 14 days. Planners can also evaluate poverty risk scores and see which districts require priority intervention."
- **Visual:** Planner dashboard tabs: Overview, Delays by Programme & Province, Hotspots matrix, 30-District Interactive Map, and Poverty Risk Calculator.

### 1:00 – 2:15 | The Core Ecosystem Flow: Farmer, Family, and Bridge Credit
- **Narrative:** 
  1. *Farmer:* "First, a smallholder farmer logs in to list 100 kg of dry beans. The system automatically benchmarks the price against the NISR median (800 RWF), flagging it as Fair."
  2. *Family:* "Next, Amina from Kirehe logs in. Her VUP transfer is 8 days late. The app dynamically opens a bridge credit limit based on her family size and days late. She buys the beans on credit without paying cash."
  3. *Farmer & Pool:* "The farmer marks the order delivered, and the community revolving social pool immediately disburses guaranteed payment to the farmer's mobile money."
  4. *Planner Settlement:* "Finally, when the VUP government disbursement wave arrives, the planner clicks 'Simulate VUP Payments Arriving', and Amina's credit is seamlessly repaid to the pool!"
- **Visual:** Walk through Farmer adding listing -> Family buying on credit -> Farmer confirming delivery -> Planner triggering VUP repayment.

### 2:15 – 2:45 | Ethical Shopper & Account Roles
- **Narrative:** "How is the pool maintained? Everyday urban consumers shop for fresh produce directly from farmers, setting an optional 1% to 5% donation share that directly refills the social pool. The app tracks exactly how many days of food each shopper has subsidized."
- **Visual:** Shopper view adjusting donation slider, buying produce, and viewing impact counter. Demonstrate the one-click demo login switcher.

### 2:45 – 3:00 | Conclusion, Ethical Limits, and Impact
- **Narrative:** "Isoko Bridge illustrates patterns without assuming reasons, respects personal data privacy, and uses a simulated zero-risk financial prototype. It bridges food security for families, ensures income for farmers, and provides evidence for planners. Thank you."
- **Visual:** Declaration page and concluding summary screen.
